Questions executives ask before adopting AI.

Is OtonomiQ replacing our PMS?

No. OtonomiQ is designed as an intelligence and execution layer that operates on top of existing PMS, POS, and CRM systems of record.

Does OtonomiQ make decisions autonomously?

AI recommends actions and coordinates execution, while human approval remains strictly in control for financial or policy-binding decisions.

Can Hotel OS be configured for our specific property policies?

Yes. Configuration adapts to your brand standards, operational rules, role authorizations, and approval thresholds.

How do we start a pilot?

Start with a single high-impact workflow (e.g., guest communications or housekeeping dispatch), prove efficiency gains, and scale across departments.
