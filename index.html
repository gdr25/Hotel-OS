How OtonomiQ Works

FROM SIGNAL TO ACTION.

01 / Guest Signal

Guest sends a message via WhatsApp, Web, or In-Room Voice asking for extra towels, late checkout, or dining recommendations.

02 / AI Intent & Context Understanding

OtonomiQ parses the unstructured natural language, maps room context, guest status (VIP/Loyalty), SLA requirements, and staff availability.

03 / Autonomous Workflow Execution

Without human intervention, OtonomiQ dispatches tasks directly to the right department app or staff mobile terminal.

04 / Human Action & Guest Confirmation

Staff accepts and completes the task. OtonomiQ auto-notifies the guest in their native language to close the loop.
